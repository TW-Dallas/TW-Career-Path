🎓 Upcoming Train-the-Trainer Coaching Class (Overview)

  • What is it: A dedicated coaching certification class designed to equip store trainers with the tools, operational standards, and coaching rhythms to guide new hires effectively through Day 1 and upcoming Day 2 curriculum.
  • Why we need it: Great digital modules only succeed with strong in-store coaching. Without trainer alignment, store execution is inconsistent, new hires get overwhelmed, and bad habits form on shift one.
  • WIIFM (What’s In It For GMs & Stores): Higher 90-day retention, faster new-hire speed-to-productivity, fewer customer order remakes, less GM burnout from constant retraining, and confident shift runners.
  • The Retention Philosophy:
    “Hiring fills the schedule; great training builds the team. When we coach our people to succeed from day one, retention follows, morale rises, and better candidates come looking for us.”
    • 69% Higher Retention: Team members who experience structured new-hire training are 69% more likely to stay with the company for up to 3 years (SHRM Foundation / Dr. Talya Bauer).
    • 50% Faster Productivity: Standardized training yields 50% greater new-hire productivity (SHRM), sharply reducing early mistakes and waste.
    • Turnover Cost Protection: In restaurant and hospitality operations, productivity loss during early ramp-up accounts for 52% of total turnover costs (averaging over $5,800 per lost hourly employee) (Cornell Center for Hospitality Research, Tracey & Hinkin).
    • Candidate Referrals: Team members who feel confident and supported from day one are nearly 3x more likely to refer friends and family for open positions (Gallup Workplace).
