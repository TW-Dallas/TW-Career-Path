CSR / CSR Captain

Wages: Hourly

The CSR delivers great service by taking orders, making food, and keeping the store clean and organized.
The CSR Captain leads CSRs on shift by setting the pace for service, accuracy, and teamwork. Expected to train new personnel

Driver / Driver Captain

Wages: Hourly

The Delivery Expert safely delivers orders on time while providing friendly service and supporting in-store operations & cleaning.
The Driver Captain guides the delivery team to meet service times, safety and operational standards, and is reponsible for training new drivers.

MIT (Manager In Training)

Wages: Hourly + Bonus

The Shift Lead runs successful shifts by managing people, product quality, and service standards.
The Assistant Manager Supports store operations by developing team members and helping drive sales and performance.

GMIT / Sr. GMIT

Wages: Hourly + Bonus

The General Manager in Training trains to lead a store by learning operations, people leadership, and business results.
The Senior General Manager in Training demonstrates readiness for GM by consistently executing strong leadership, team development, and consistent operational excellence.

GM

Wages: Salary + Bonus

The General Manager leads the store by developing managers, driving results, and delivering great customer experiences.

Do-IT

Wages: Salary + Bonus

The DO-it program helps to prepare high-performing managers to tackle a supervisory role. Here, managers will learn multi-store leadership by supporting General Managers and improving store performance.

DO

The Director of Operations leads multiple stores by coaching GMs, driving standards, and achieving business goals.

VP

Franchisee

DO-IT Program Requirements

I. Prerequisites

  • ✅ DO Sponsorship: Must have formal approval from your District Manager.
  • ✅ Training: Must have completed all MIT Level 1, 2, and 3 classes and corresponding certifications.
  • ✅ Driver Eligible: Must be cleared to assist with deliveries if required.

II. Performance Data (Last Quarter)

  • 📈 Ops Metrics: Have your Sales, ADT, Food Cost, and Labor/Load data ready.
  • 📉 Efficiency: Know your Inside Hour Waste average.
  • 📋 Scores: Have your last 3 OA scores listed.

III. Leadership & Review

  • 💼 Financials: Be prepared to discuss your last 3 P&Ls and Bonuses (wins/variances).
  • 👥 Development: List specific GMs/AMs you have mentored.
  • ⭐ VOC: Be ready to discuss recent customer feedback trends.

* Disclaimer: The DO-IT Program is an internship. Participation does not guarantee promotion or pay increase. Continued participation is contingent on performance.
