## 📌 [Draft: Next Wow Way Huddle Edition]
*Scheduled: Thursday @ 9:00 AM CST/CDT (Compiled @ 5:00 AM CST/CDT)*

### 🍕 Fresh Out of the Oven (New Features & Content)
* **Module 3 Menu Overview — Domino™ Crust Rollout:** Added the brand-new Domino™ Detroit Style Parmesan crust across all 3 interactive foundations slides:
  * **Crust Overview & Guidelines:** Features official menu photo reference, rectangular pan definition, two layers of cheese with signature garlic seasoning, and the crucial **3 toppings maximum** restriction.
  * **Size Constraints:** Highlights that Domino™ comes in **one size only** (with its own dedicated POS button).
  * **Default Cut Standard:** Added the official cut specification showing Domino™ is **cut in half vertically** (2 halves), complete with a custom matching visual diagram.
* **Tactical Assistance System & Trainer Authorization (POS Practice):** Trainees experiencing roadblocks during live phone order practice can now request targeted on-screen guidance without getting stuck:
  * **Tiered Assistance Engine:** Trainees receive 1 Free Hint (animated blue radar pulse over target screen region) followed by a 2nd Hint (-2% deduction). For direct button answers, trainees can request a Direct Answer (-5% deduction) which illuminates the exact button with a pulsating red spotlight.
  * **Trainer PIN Authorization:** Once assistance allowances are exhausted, additional hints and direct answers require on-duty Trainer or GM 4-digit PIN authorization (-5% deduction per assist).
  * **Trainer PIN Abuse Watchdog:** Built-in safeguards automatically monitor and flag suspicious PIN overrides (such as consecutive step overrides or excessive bypasses on a single trainee evaluation) to ensure authentic, hands-on learning.
  * **Physical Execution Guarantee:** Unlike automated skips, assistance never advances the screen automatically—trainees must still physically locate, click, or type the required order details themselves.
* **1-Click Field Focus in POS Practice:** Clicking directly on customer detail fields (such as phone number, address number, street name, or unit/suite) now immediately focuses and opens typing mode in 1 click, eliminating redundant clicks and streamlining order entry flow.
* **Live Phone Order Practice in the POS Simulator:** Trainees can now tackle realistic, full-speed live phone order practice! Features live customer audio recordings, interactive telephone hardware training (Park vs. Mute), on-screen "Put on Hold" and "Rewind 5s" controls, multiline delivery instructions, complex multi-item pizzas with half-and-half toppings, sides, drinks, rewards gating, and end-of-stage performance scorecards with Coach Mike.
* **Printable New Hire Training Resources (Resource Library):**
  * **New Hire Training Program FAQ & Visual Reference Guide:** Released a store-ready, 2-page printable job aid covering key onboarding FAQs, step-by-step visual credential guides, and QR codes for the Training Hub and Trainer PIN generator. Available directly in the [Resource Library](https://tw-dallas.github.io/TW-Career-Path/learning_hub.html) under Training!
  * **Pulse New Employee Setup Guide (Managers Library):** Added the official step-by-step Pulse new employee setup guide directly to the Managers section of the Resource Library, ranked #1 for fast GM and Shift Runner access.
  * **New Hire Trainer & Trainee Packets (Day 1):** Official Day 1 Trainer coaching checklists and Trainee workbooks are live in the Resource Library.
* **Video Quality & Dynamic LEADS Quiz Refresh:** Upgraded Day 1 orientation with high-definition video and normalized audio, refreshed Module 1 knowledge check questions, and deployed an 8-question rotating LEADS scenario quiz bank in Module 2.

* **Day 1 Trainee Report Card Precision & Duplicate Prevention:** Shift report cards are strictly locked to final Module 5 sign-off, certified seals remain permanently saved upon review, and automatic server-side deduplication prevents duplicate emails on the same shift.
* **Authentic Active Seat Timers & Rapid Completion Alerts:** Replaced estimated durations with authentic active seat timers across all modules, and added automatic leadership alerts if a shift is certified in under 45 minutes.
* **Learning Hub Lobby Card Photography & Dynamic Layout:** Replaced placeholder icons with high-res store photography (`LMS_Cover.jpg` and `Library_cover.png`) optimized for fast store Wi-Fi, added dynamic zero-scroll responsive scaling, and introduced a `"← Back to Lobby"` navigation link on the Training Dashboard navbar.
* **Top-Header "🛠️ Report Issue" Button & Automated Confirmation Receipts:** Moved the issue report trigger neatly into the top navigation header and integrated instant branded email receipts sent back to the team member with the store GM CC'd.
* **Typo-Tolerant Address Entry & Auto-Resume:** Added smart typo tolerance and live street dropdown matching to the POS simulator, along with silent employee-isolated auto-resume if a workstation refreshes.
