Play #3: The LEADS Playbook

When mistakes happen, we don't get defensive. We run LEADS:

  • Listen to understand without interrupting.
  • Empathize with how the customer feels.
  • Apologize with sincere ownership.
  • Do what it takes with an immediate solution.
  • Stand by your promise through personal follow-through.
