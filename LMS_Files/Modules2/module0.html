Play #3: The LEADS Playbook

When mistakes happen, we don't get defensive. We run LEADS:

  • Listen: Understand the real issue without interrupting.
  • Empathize: Connect with how the customer feels.
  • Apologize: Sincere ownership builds trust.
  • Do what it takes: Provide an immediate solution.
  • Stand by your promise: Personal follow-through.
