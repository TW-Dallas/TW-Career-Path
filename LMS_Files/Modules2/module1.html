📓 Physical Playbook Film Study Notes

🔴 1. The Defensive Fumble
0:46
Play Fumble Tape

"Well, it's Friday night and two of our drivers called out, so there wasn't really anything we could do about it. If you want a fresh one, you'll have to drive up here and pick it up yourself, otherwise you'll just have to reheat it."

✗ Personal Foul:

Blaming staffing and rush conditions makes the customer feel dismissed and abandoned.

🟢 2. The Winning Play
1:06
Play Winning Tape

"Oh no, I am so sorry about that! I completely understand how frustrating that is, especially on a school night with hungry kids... Since it's getting late, I am going to credit your entire order back to your phone number—and I'm adding an extra $10 credit on top so dinner is on us next time."

✓ Key Coaching Takeaway:

Solves the customer's actual time problem, over-delivers on store credit, and secures lifelong brand loyalty.

Store Decision Guide

Remake vs. Store Credit — Calling the right play every time.

🍕⚡

When to REMAKE

  • Guest wants dinner tonight and has time to wait.
  • Mark as a Priority Remake on the makeline.
  • For deliveries: Rush a driver immediately with fresh hot items.
💳✨

When to CREDIT

  • Guest already ate or doesn't have time for a remake.
  • Apply store credit directly to their phone profile in the POS.
  • Always confirm the credit amount and thank them sincerely!

🏆 The Golden Rule of Recovery

Never make the customer feel like an inconvenience. When a mistake happens, taking complete ownership and executing the LEADS playbook turns an upset customer into a dedicated brand advocate for life!
