Q-TIP: Quit Taking It Personally

Coach Mike's Gold Standard Mindset

When a customer calls or comes up to the counter angry, they aren't mad at you as a person—they are hungry, tired, and disappointed that their dinner went wrong.

  • The Defensive Trap: Saying "I didn't make your pizza" or "We're short-staffed" only makes the customer angrier.
  • The Q-TIP Solution: Put on your emotional armor, stay calm, and focus 100% of your energy on being the problem solver.

Store Decision Guide

Remake vs. Store Credit — Calling the right play every time.

🍕⚡

When to REMAKE

  • Customer wants dinner tonight and has time for a fresh remake.
  • Mark as a Priority Remake in the makeline queue.
  • For deliveries: Rush a driver out immediately with hot food and any forgotten sauces/drinks.
💳✨

When to CREDIT

  • Customer already ate, or doesn't have time to wait for a remake.
  • Apply a store credit directly to their phone number profile in the POS.
  • Always confirm the credit amount and thank them sincerely for letting us know!
