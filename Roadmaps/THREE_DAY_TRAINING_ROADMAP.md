# 🍕 Team Wow 3-Day LMS Training Program: Master Architecture & Content Blueprint

---

## 📌 1. Program Overview & System Architecture

The Team Wow 3-Day LMS Training Program is designed to guide new hires through an alternating cadence of **digital LMS modules** and **hands-on in-person store experiences with trainers**. This structure eliminates screen fatigue, keeps trainers actively engaged, and ensures real-world muscle memory.

## 📅 2. Day-by-Day Master Breakdown

---

### 🍕 DAY 1: Phone Training, Customer Interaction & POS Navigation
* **Audience:** All New Hires (Universal for Drivers & CSRs)
* **Target Time:** 3 – 4 Hours
* **Primary Goal:** Build unwavering confidence answering phones, navigating the POS system, taking accurate orders, and delivering outstanding customer service.

| Stage | Activity | Type | Description & Learning Objectives |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Exp 0** | **The Welcome** | In-Person | Greeting, uniform image check, clock-in procedure, escort to training station. |
| **Mod 1** | **Introduction & System Setup** | Online | LMS check-in, dynamic avatar creator, Brent welcome video, Team Wow history & mission, safety/anti-harassment basics, 7-10 question knowledge check. |
| **Exp 1** | **Orientation & Store Tour** | In-Person | Store walkthrough, schedule board, time-off requests, tardy/call-out protocols. |
| **Mod 2** | **Customer Service Basics** | Online | Service expectations, tone of voice, active listening, foundational frameworks (**LEADS, ACE, Q-TIP**), knowledge check. |
| **Exp 2** | **Basic Interactions & Roleplay** | In-Person | In-person roleplay scenarios: "In a Hurry Walk-in", "Mumbled Speakerphone Call", "Wrong Store Pickup", and the "Lifeline Script" (*"I am new and still learning..."*). |
| **Mod 3** | **Introduction to Product & Menu** | Online | Menu overview slideshow, interactive "Match the Product" drill. |
| **Exp 3** | **Menu Review** | In-Person | Physical paper menu review, job aid locations, visual product layout. |
| **Mod 4** | **The POS Simulator** | Online | 2-ring rule, phone greeting script, 3-stage interactive order entry simulator (Guided Clickable ➔ Paced Audio ➔ Real-time Live Fire calls). |
| **Exp 4** | **Advanced Roleplay & Sign-Off** | In-Person | Trainer-led live mock orders, upselling practice, payment processing, wait time quoting, difficult customer "Boss Fight". |
| **Mod 5** | **Recap & Trainer Authorization** | Online | Big picture review, secure Trainer PIN entry unlocking Day 2. |

---

### ☀️ DAY 2: Shared Morning Block (Universal: CSR & Driver)
* **Audience:** Both Drivers and CSRs begin Day 2 with these shared foundational modules.
* **Target Time:** ~45 – 60 Minutes

#### 🔹 Module 0: Day 1 Morning Warm-Up & Retention Quiz (Online)
* **Purpose:** Quick confidence builder to verify Day 1 retention.
* **Core Topics:** Phone greeting script, carryout vs delivery workflow steps, core menu items, basic safety standards.
* **Kickoff Video Script (Coach Bobby - 3-Game Series):**
  > *"All right, team! Welcome back to Day 2!*
  > 
  > *Game 1 is officially in the win column, and you came out swinging on Day 1. You suited up in your game day jersey, learned what it means to be part of Team Wow, tackled the phone lines, and mastered the playbook inside our POS simulator.*
  > 
  > *Now, welcome to Game 2 of the series. Today’s gameplan is going to follow that same winning rhythm: we’ll break down the film here on screen, and then you’ll take the floor for live, hands-on reps with your coach.*
  > 
  > *Today, the playbook expands. We’re branching out into position-specific specialty drills—so whether you’re heading out on the road as a delivery driver or taking command of the inside makeline as a CSR, you’re going to learn the exact skills for your position.*
  > 
  > *Before we run today’s plays, we need to make sure those Day 1 fundamentals are locked down. So grab your playbooks, lock in, and listen up for a quick film review. Stay sharp, because right on the other side of this tape is a quick warm-up quiz before we kick off Day 2.*
  > 
  > *Let’s get after it!"*
* **Interactive Element:** 7–10 randomized questions with avatar streak bonuses.

#### 🔹 Module 1: The Art of Customer Recovery — LEADS & Q-TIP in Action (Online 🎧)
* **Purpose:** Master de-escalation, conflict resolution, and accountability.
* **Key Philosophies:**
  * **🛡️ Q-TIP (Quit Taking It Personally):** Emotional armor for phones and doorsteps. Hungry customers aren't attacking the team member personally; they are frustrated with their dinner. Drop defense, stay calm, and become the problem solver.
  * **🔄 Mindset Flip Drill:** Identifying defensive traps (*"I didn't make the pizza!"*) vs. solution-oriented responses (*"I completely understand, let's get this fixed immediately"*).
* **Interactive Audio Scenarios:**
  * **Clip 1 (`Customer_Concern_Credit.mp3`): "Deconstruct the Play"**
    * *Scenario:* Customer asks for manager after a carryout mix-up; team member professionally de-escalates and offers help directly.
    * *Mechanic:* Audio auto-pauses after key dialogue; student identifies which LEADS step was demonstrated.
  * **Clip 2 (`Customer_Concern_Credit_2.mp3`): "Call the Shot"**
    * *Scenario:* Customer calls regarding a wrong delivery order.
    * *Mechanic:* Audio pauses *before* team member responds; student chooses the best response among multiple choices. When submitted, the audio resumes with Bobby saying their exact choice!
  * **Decision Tree Guide:** When to offer an immediate remake vs. store credit vs. refund policy.

#### 🔹 Module 2: Cleaning, Chemical Safety & Sanitation (Online)
* **Purpose:** Food safety standards, pristine store environment, cross-contamination prevention.
* **Core Topics:** Handwashing 20-second rule, glove usage triggers, 3-compartment sink setup (Wash / Rinse / Sanitize) & PPM testing, mop sink, chore board routine.
* **Interactive Mini-Game:** Drag-and-drop sink setup order + chemical bottle identification.

#### 🔹 Experience 1: Hands-on Cleaning Tour & Sinks (In-Person with Trainer)
* Sani-bucket preparation with test strips, 3-compartment sink setup, sweep/mop demo, chemical safety sheet walk-through, chore chart introduction.

### 🚗 DAY 2: DRIVER TRACK (Branch A)
* **Target Time:** 4 – 8 Hours (combines modules + road ride-alongs)
* **Primary Goal:** Master driver safety, delivery technology, cash-drop protocols, and execute first customer deliveries via ride-alongs.

| Stage | Activity | Type | Description & Learning Objectives |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mod 3** | **Driver Safety & Delivery Technology** | Online | Safe driving standards, Drivosity scoring criteria (acceleration, braking, cornering, speeding) & rewards, Delivery Experience App setup, Cash Drop procedures, Car-Side delivery standards. |
| **Exp 2** | **Car-Side & Ride-Along Experience** | In-Person | Set up driver app, bank ($20 change), illuminated car top sign. Check bags for dip cups/drinks/sides. Customer interaction at the door. Excess cash box drops. Complete 3 ride-alongs with trainer, then swap to driver seat. |
| **Mod 4** | **Driver Day 2 Wrap-Up & Sign-Off** | Online | Review: *"You are the face of Team Wow outside the store. Hot, Fast, Accurate, and Friendly."* Trainer enters secure PIN unlocking Day 3 Driver Track. |

---

### 🍕 DAY 2: CSR / INSIDER TRACK (Branch B)
* **Target Time:** 4 – 8 Hours
* **Primary Goal:** Master Ready-To-Use (RTU) prep, dough preparation for sides, makeline ergonomics, and topping portion standards.

| Stage | Activity | Type | Description & Learning Objectives |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mod 3** | **RTU / RTE Procedures & Morning Prep** | Online | Pizza sauce bucket dilution and mixing procedures, Ready-to-Eat (RTE) handling, Pan Pizza & Bread Bites prep (pan oiling, dough stretching, cutting), Day-Dot labeling & shelf-life management. |
| **Exp 2** | **Hands-on Prep Practice** | In-Person | Location of prep supplies, mixing 1–2 test buckets of sauce with trainer, prepping test pans and orders of parmesan bread bites. |
| **Mod 4** | **Product Training & The Makeline** | Online | Left-to-right makeline layout, topping codes (e.g., P, S, H, K, M, R, C), portion cups, portion scale calibration, anatomy of a 5-Star pizza. Interactive "Name that Topping & Portions" drill. |
| **Exp 3** | **Makeline Hands-on Walkthrough** | In-Person | Left-to-right ingredient tour, scale and portion cup training, "Name that Topping" speed drill, paired topping practice with skilled trainer. |
| **Mod 5** | **CSR Day 2 Wrap-Up & Sign-Off** | Online | Review: *"You are responsible for what the customer receives. Know it, build it, sell it."* Trainer enters secure PIN unlocking Day 3 CSR Track. |

### 🏁 DAY 3: MASTER LEVEL & FINAL SIGN-OFF

---

#### 🚗 DAY 3: DRIVER TRACK
* **Primary Goal:** Complete full order cycle with speed and safety, learn secondary prep skills (sauce/bites), and master OA (Operations Audit) delivery standards.

| Stage | Activity | Type | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mod 0** | **Day 2 Retention Quiz** | Online | Driver safety, Drivosity rules, cash handling, and customer resolution. |
| **Mod 1** | **Driver RTU / Prep Basics** | Online | Sauce preparation and bread bite prep so drivers can assist during morning/pre-rush. |
| **Exp 1** | **Hands-on Prep** | In-Person | Prepare 1-2 sauce buckets and bread bites with trainer. |
| **Mod 2** | **Operations Audit (OA) Overview & HTA** | Online | What is OA and why scoring matters, visual guide to passing vs failing product, HTA (Hustle to Arm / Smart Hustle), Bag & Tag, DSS, pre-bagging sides/drinks. |
| **Exp 2** | **Follow the Order & Road Execution** | In-Person | Follow an order from phone ring through makeline, oven, bagging, and delivery. Execute solo/observed delivery runs. |
| **Mod 3** | **Final Driver Sign-Off & Graduation** | Online | Comprehensive evaluation checklist. Final Trainer PIN unlocks regular scheduling graduation status! |

#### 🍕 DAY 3: CSR / INSIDER TRACK
* **Primary Goal:** Execute high-speed makeline assembly during peak hours, master 5-star pizza standards, understand OA scoring, and complete solo order fulfillment.

| Stage | Activity | Type | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

| **Mod 1** | **Operations Audit (OA) Overview** | Online | OA inspection criteria, product grading (crust, rim, portion, bake, cut), photo gallery of 5-star vs remake-level pizzas. |
| **Exp 1** | **5-Star Makeline Mastery & HTA** | In-Person | Reading makeline tickets at speed, 2-handed topping technique, cheese distribution, scale verification, HTA bagging & tagging. |
| **Mod 2** | **Final CSR Sign-Off & Graduation** | Online | Comprehensive evaluation checklist. Final Trainer PIN unlocks regular scheduling graduation status! |
